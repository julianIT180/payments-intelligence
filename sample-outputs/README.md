# Sample outputs

Raw generated briefs are **not committed here yet**, by choice.

The two behaviours a reader would want to see from a sample — a well-evidenced brief
that publishes, and a fabricated subject that is refused — are already shown, from real
runs, in three places that cannot be quietly edited after the fact:

- **The live site** — [payments-intelligence.vercel.app](https://payments-intelligence.vercel.app/):
  the digital euro brief with its tiered source list and `[S#]` citations is on the feed.
- **Screenshots** — [`docs/img/`](../docs/img/) (`feed.png`, `brief-digital-euro.png`).
- **The validation record** — [`docs/evaluation.md`](../docs/evaluation.md) and the
  machine-checked fixtures in [`evaluation/`](../evaluation/), including the adversarial
  "Veltrix Pay" refusal (fabricated company, three topic-adjacent sources survived
  triage, model still declined).

A committed brief here would just be a frozen copy of what the feed already shows. When
it is worth pinning specific runs — a representative brief and a verbatim refusal
transcript — they will be added unedited. Until then, nothing in this directory is a
placeholder pretending to be a result.
